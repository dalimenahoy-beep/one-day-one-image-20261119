# The World in One Picture — Thursday, 19 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun bounty **"Sum up 19 November's world news in one
image"** ([news-image-20261119](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119)).

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) copies the poster
`entry.png` held in this repository into the output directory
(`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, default `/output`) as `entry.png`, byte for byte,
so every run writes identical bytes.

The poster is a newspaper front page for **Thursday, 19 November 2026**,
typeset from `poster.html` (rendered to PNG with headless Chromium so every
word is crisp and correctly spelled — no AI image generation). It is
1024 × 1024 px and about 197 KiB, inside the contest limits of
1024 × 1024 px and 1 MiB.

## The stories chosen

Researched on 19 November 2026 at about 04:00 UTC, while the day was still
in progress, from the stories most widely reported by then:

1. **Lead — Trump, Xi & Putin meet face to face.** The US, Chinese and
   Russian presidents hold their first-ever trilateral meeting on the
   sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen, with nuclear arms, Ukraine
   and Iran in focus.
2. **Anthropic's $81.3bn IPO is the biggest ever.** The AI lab behind
   Claude priced its debut at $83 a share, raising $81.3 billion, and
   starts trading on the Nasdaq today at a valuation of about
   $1.5 trillion.
3. **Gaza — Israel launches a rare ground incursion.** Israeli tanks cross
   the ceasefire line; Australia, Canada, Italy and Britain urge Israel to
   let aid reach flooded areas of Gaza.
4. **Washington — Jeffries set to be first Black Speaker.** US Democrats
   nominate Hakeem Jeffries to lead the House of Representatives.
5. **Ukraine — Crimean Bridge attacked again.** Russia says traffic on
   the Kerch Bridge is halted after a new attack; the EU proposes a
   €45 billion loan for Kyiv.
6. **Space — Voyager 1 is one light-day away.** NASA's 1977 probe is now
   so far from Earth that its radio signals take a full day to arrive.
7. **India — Cyclone Akvan nears the east coast.** The storm is expected
   to cross the coast between Machilipatnam and Puri.
8. **Culture — GTA VI finally launches worldwide.** Gamers pour into Vice
   City as the game begins its global rollout after a 13-year wait; a
   Van Gogh painting sells at auction for a record $190.4m.
